Questions before we begin

Know what
comes next.

Can you improve an existing system?+

Yes. We first review the existing implementation, access and documentation. We then propose the smallest useful change and identify any dependencies before estimating the work.

How are timelines and pricing agreed?+

After discovery, we provide a proposal based on deliverables, integrations, review requirements and ongoing support. A pilot can be scoped separately when the approach needs validation.

What happens after launch?+

You receive documentation and a practical handover. Monitoring, maintenance and further improvements are covered by an explicitly agreed support arrangement.

AI customer support

A useful AI support assistant knows when to hand over

Design the transition to your team as carefully as the automated answer.

Jolpai editorial · 14 September 2026 · 2 min read

In this guide

Define the supported conversationPass context, not just a ticketUse content someone maintainsEvaluate the whole experience

Define the supported conversation

Start with recurring questions that have approved answers. Identify requests that need account access, judgment or a policy exception. The assistant should know the boundary between explaining information and taking action. A support experience becomes frustrating when it confidently promises something that the business cannot deliver.

Pass context, not just a ticket

When a person takes over, include the customer’s question, what the assistant already checked and what remains unresolved. Avoid asking the customer to repeat the whole conversation. Give the receiving team a clear queue and status so the handover has an owner. If support is unavailable, explain the next step honestly.

Use content someone maintains

An assistant can repeat an outdated policy very efficiently. Assign responsibility for reviewing source content and removing conflicting instructions. Test common questions after meaningful policy changes. Where possible, let users see the supporting information so they can understand the answer and staff can diagnose mistakes.

Evaluate the whole experience

Review unresolved requests, inappropriate answers and successful handovers, not only the number of conversations handled automatically. Start with a limited topic set and expand after examining real examples. Customer satisfaction depends on reaching a useful resolution, whether that resolution comes from the assistant or a person.

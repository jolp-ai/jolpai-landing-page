Something else on your mind?
Just ask us ↗

Where should we start with AI?+

Start with one repetitive task that takes time or creates avoidable mistakes. We help you assess whether automation is suitable, what information it needs and how you will measure a useful result.

Can you work with our existing tools?+

That is our starting point. We review the tools you already use and their integration options before recommending any changes. Availability depends on their APIs, permissions and subscription plans.

Do we need a technical team?+

No. We explain the options in plain language, agree on a manageable scope and provide documentation and a handover. We will still need someone in your business who understands the process and can approve decisions.

How much does a project cost?+

Pricing depends on the workflow, integrations, data and support required. After discovery, we provide a scoped proposal that separates implementation fees from any ongoing platform or usage costs.

Will AI make decisions without our approval?+

We agree on those boundaries together. Sensitive actions can require approval, and uncertain cases can go to a person. We design escalation and fallback paths around the consequences of getting something wrong.

How do we work together?+

Yes. Jolpai works remotely with growing businesses. We agree on meeting overlap, communication, data handling and support arrangements before starting.
